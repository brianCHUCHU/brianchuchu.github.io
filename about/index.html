About

I’m a master’s student in Management Science & Engineering at Stanford, interested in applied AI, forecasting, and how people make decisions with imperfect information. I studied International Business at National Taiwan University, where I developed an interest in integrating data science with business decision-making. I enjoy taking an open-ended problem and building something useful around it.

Before Stanford, I led the AI Innovation & Support Center at Chang Gung University. Working with faculty and a small developer team, I built a shared platform for bringing AI agents into classrooms, from understanding teaching needs to developing tools and supporting their rollout. That experience shaped how I approach technical work: start with the people using it, understand their constraints, and keep improving after launch.

My research has explored coordination in multi-agent reinforcement learning and Bayesian forecasting for sparse demand.

I’m currently exploring research opportunities at Stanford and summer internships where I can contribute to building and evaluating AI systems.

Always happy to meet new people, exchange ideas, and learn what you’re working on.
