About

I am an M.S. student in Management Science and Engineering at Stanford University. I work on machine learning, forecasting, and software for research and education.

At Chang Gung University, I built the technical foundation of a new AI Innovation and Support Center and led CGU SAGE, a shared platform for faculty AI projects. The work combined product and infrastructure development with hands-on delivery from problem scoping through classroom rollout.

My earlier work includes product analytics at GliaCloud, content automation at Gogolook, and applied machine-learning capstones with momo.com and 55688 through the Business Analytics credit program at National Taiwan University.

CGU SAGE and its related assets belong to Chang Gung University; this site describes only my role and public work.
