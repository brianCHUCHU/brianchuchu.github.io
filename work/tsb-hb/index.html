TSB-HB

A hierarchical Bayesian extension of TSB for sparse and cold-start demand series.

Role
Equal-contribution co-author and co-developer
Owner / collaborators
Zong-Han Bai and Po-Yen Chu

Overview

Intermittent demand creates a difficult combination: sparse observations, cold-start items, and possible obsolescence. Classical TSB forecasting offers a useful heuristic, but related items cannot share evidence within the model.

TSB-HB recasts the problem hierarchically. Taxonomy-conditioned priors provide partial pooling across items, stabilizing estimates for sparse series while preserving item-level heterogeneity.

Evaluation

The equal-contribution preprint by Zong-Han Bai and Po-Yen Chu reports point and probabilistic forecasting experiments on UCI Online Retail and an M5 sample. The accompanying Python repository includes the implementation, dataset loaders, baselines, experiments, and diagnostics.

My contribution

I worked on the model, experiments, code, and paper. Zong-Han Bai and I are equal-contribution co-authors.
