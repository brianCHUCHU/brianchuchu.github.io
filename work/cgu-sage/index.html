CGU SAGE

A teaching platform and public Agent Starter built for Chang Gung University, with access control, usage visibility, and instructor workflows.

Role
Founding center lead; product and technical leadership
Owner / collaborators
Chang Gung University

Overview

CGU SAGE is a teaching platform built for Chang Gung University. It supports instructor workflows while making access, usage, cost, and data boundaries visible.

As founding lead of Chang Gung University’s AI Innovation and Support Center, I helped establish the center and led product and technical work on the platform with a student developer team.

My role

My work included product design, platform architecture, access control, usage and cost logging, and faculty demonstrations. I also worked with faculty to turn teaching needs into scoped pilots.

Public reference implementation

I designed and built the CGU SAGE Agent Starter: reviewed Node.js and FastAPI reference implementations for self-hosted agents. The public repository shows the integration pattern without exposing CGU’s private platform code or operational data.

The repository is publicly viewable source governed by the CGU Internal Use License. It is not an open-source personal project.

Ownership and disclosure

CGU SAGE is an institutional project of Chang Gung University. This case study describes my role and public demonstrations. CGU’s source, product assets, and related materials remain Chang Gung University’s property.

This portfolio does not republish private source, internal data, credentials, configuration, learner information, or restricted screenshots.
